## Company Incorporation Costs

The cost of incorporating a company can include the applicable government registration fee as well as professional service fees if an incorporation service provider is used.

Additional requirements may apply depending on the company's structure and circumstances.

Entrepreneurs should consider both mandatory government costs and professional service costs when planning their incorporation budget.

## Company Secretary Costs

A Singapore company generally needs to maintain a company secretary.

Professional corporate secretarial services may involve an annual fee depending on the provider and services included.

The scope of service can vary considerably between providers.

Some packages may cover basic statutory compliance, while others may include additional corporate secretarial support.

## Accounting Costs

Accounting costs can vary based on the size and complexity of the business.

Factors that can influence accounting costs include:

- Number of transactions
- Monthly transaction volume
- Payroll requirements
- Accounting software
- Financial reporting requirements
- Inventory
- Number of bank accounts
- Business structure
- Additional accounting services

A small company with limited transactions may have significantly different accounting requirements from a growing business.

## Tax Filing Costs

Companies may need professional assistance with corporate tax computation and filing.

Tax service costs can depend on:

- Company size
- Accounting records
- Complexity of transactions
- Tax adjustments
- Foreign income
- Tax deductions
- Supporting documentation
- Additional tax advisory requirements

Businesses with more complex tax positions should budget accordingly.

## Annual Compliance Costs

Maintaining a Singapore company involves recurring compliance responsibilities.

These may include:

- Annual return filing
- Corporate secretarial services
- Financial statements
- Accounting
- Tax filing
- Other statutory compliance requirements

These are recurring costs rather than one-time incorporation expenses.

## Mandatory vs Optional Costs

When planning a company setup budget, it is useful to distinguish between mandatory costs and optional professional services.

Potential mandatory costs may include applicable government fees.

Professional services may include:

- Company incorporation assistance
- Corporate secretarial services
- Accounting
- Tax filing
- Bookkeeping
- Registered office services
- Nominee director services where applicable

Not every company will require every service.

## First-Year Cost vs Ongoing Cost

The first year of operating a company can have different costs from subsequent years.

First-year expenses may include incorporation and initial setup costs.

Ongoing expenses may include:

- Accounting
- Corporate secretarial services
- Tax filing
- Annual compliance
- Bookkeeping
- Payroll
- Other business support services

Business owners should therefore distinguish between initial setup costs and recurring annual expenses.

## Important Considerations

The cost of operating a Singapore company can depend on factors including:

- Company structure
- Business activity
- Number of transactions
- Accounting complexity
- Tax requirements
- Corporate secretarial requirements
- Number of employees
- Payroll requirements
- Registered office requirements
- Foreign ownership
- Local director requirements
- Nominee director requirements
- Additional professional services

For this reason, there is no single fixed cost that applies to every Singapore company.
