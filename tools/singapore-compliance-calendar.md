A free Singapore business compliance calendar designed to help company directors, founders and SMEs understand recurring corporate, tax, GST and financial reporting deadlines.

## What Does the Calendar Cover?

Depending on the information provided, the calendar can help identify areas such as:

- Annual Return requirements
- Corporate tax obligations
- GST filing obligations
- AGM related requirements
- Financial reporting
- Other recurring company compliance tasks

The exact obligations and deadlines that apply can depend on factors such as the company's financial year end, GST registration status, company type and other circumstances.

## Why Singapore Companies Need a Compliance Calendar

Singapore companies have multiple statutory and tax obligations throughout the year.

Missing one deadline can create unnecessary administrative work, penalties or compliance issues.

A calendar provides a simple way to see upcoming obligations instead of relying on memory or checking different requirements separately.

## Important Compliance Areas

### Annual Return

Singapore companies generally need to file an Annual Return with ACRA within the applicable statutory deadline.

The filing requirements and timing depend on the company's circumstances.

### Corporate Tax

Companies may have corporate income tax filing and payment obligations administered by IRAS.

The applicable deadlines depend on the company's tax obligations and filing requirements.

### GST

GST registered businesses have periodic GST return filing and payment obligations.

The filing frequency and deadlines depend on the company's GST reporting cycle.

### Financial Statements

Companies may need to prepare financial statements and meet applicable financial reporting requirements.

The specific requirements can vary depending on the company's circumstances.
