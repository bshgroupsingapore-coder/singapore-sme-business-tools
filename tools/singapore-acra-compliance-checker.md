## What Is ACRA Compliance?

The Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) is Singapore's regulator of business registration, financial reporting and corporate compliance matters.

Singapore companies have ongoing obligations after incorporation.

These obligations can include maintaining company information, preparing financial statements, holding required meetings, filing annual returns and maintaining appropriate corporate records.

## Annual Return Filing

Singapore companies generally need to file an annual return with ACRA.

The annual return provides important information about the company and must be filed within the applicable deadline.

The filing requirements and deadlines can vary depending on the company's type and circumstances.

Companies should ensure that their corporate records and required information are kept up to date before filing.

## Financial Statements

Companies may have obligations relating to the preparation and filing of financial statements.

The requirements can depend on factors such as:

- Company type
- Financial reporting requirements
- Whether the company qualifies for audit exemption
- Applicable accounting standards
- Filing requirements under Singapore regulations

Companies should determine which financial reporting requirements apply to their specific circumstances.

## XBRL Filing

Some Singapore companies are required to file financial statements with ACRA in XBRL format.

XBRL stands for eXtensible Business Reporting Language.

The specific XBRL filing requirements can depend on the company's circumstances and applicable filing rules.

Businesses should verify the latest requirements before preparing or submitting an XBRL filing.

## Annual General Meeting

Companies may have obligations relating to holding an Annual General Meeting (AGM).

Whether an AGM is required and the applicable timing can depend on the company's circumstances and applicable Singapore company law requirements.

Companies should review their specific position rather than assuming that the same AGM requirements apply to every company.

## Corporate Secretary

Singapore companies are generally required to have a company secretary.

The company secretary plays an important role in maintaining corporate records and supporting compliance with applicable statutory requirements.

The company secretary should understand the company's filing deadlines, corporate records and recurring obligations.

## Why ACRA Compliance Matters

Missing corporate filing deadlines can create unnecessary administrative problems and may result in penalties or other regulatory consequences.

A regular compliance review can help companies:

1. Identify upcoming obligations.
2. Keep corporate records organised.
3. Track annual filing deadlines.
4. Understand financial reporting responsibilities.
5. Reduce the risk of missed statutory requirements.
6. Know when professional corporate secretarial support may be needed.

## Important Considerations

ACRA compliance can depend on factors including:

- Company type
- Company incorporation date
- Financial year end
- Company size
- Audit exemption status
- Financial reporting requirements
- AGM requirements
- Shareholding structure
- Changes to company information
- Applicable Singapore company law

Two companies may therefore have different compliance requirements even if they operate in similar industries.
