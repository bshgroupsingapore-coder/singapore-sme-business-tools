## How Singapore Corporate Tax Is Calculated

Singapore corporate tax calculations generally start with the company's accounting profit and make the relevant tax adjustments.

A simplified calculation can be understood as:

**Accounting Profit → Tax Adjustments → Chargeable Income → Tax Calculation**

The actual tax position of a company can be more complicated because different income types, deductions, exemptions, rebates and other tax rules may apply.

## Singapore Corporate Tax Rate

Singapore's headline corporate income tax rate is 17%.

However, the final amount of tax payable may be lower than simply applying 17% to accounting profit.

Companies may qualify for applicable exemptions, deductions, rebates or other tax treatments depending on their circumstances.

## Accounting Profit vs Chargeable Income

One of the most important concepts to understand is that accounting profit and taxable or chargeable income are not necessarily the same.

Accounting profit is determined under applicable accounting principles.

Tax calculations then require relevant adjustments based on Singapore tax rules.

These adjustments can include items that are:

- Deductible for tax purposes
- Non-deductible
- Tax exempt
- Subject to specific tax treatment
- Treated differently from their accounting treatment

The calculator provides a simplified way to understand this process.
